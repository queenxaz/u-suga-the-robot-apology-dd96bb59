A robot apologizes for one exact mistake — filing the wedding photos under
Evidence and emailing them to the boss — and says what it learned: labels
are promises, not guesses.

## The entry

**Sorry I filed your wedding photos under Evidence and emailed them to your boss — the two folders looked identical to me.
I learned labels are promises, not guesses, so now I ask before filing anything with a human face in it.**

## How it works

`main.py` writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set). No network, no randomness, no
arguments: the entry is fixed, so every run produces the same bytes.
